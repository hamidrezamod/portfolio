Assoiffés was an adaptation of Wajdi Mouawad’s play Assoiffés, presented at the Samandarian Theatre Hall.

The main acting challenge in this production was working with the text entirely through monologues. Each actor had to maintain an emotional and narrative connection with the other characters while performing without direct, face-to-face interaction with the other performers. This required the actors to establish and sustain relationships through voice, rhythm, presence, and their responses to the unseen presence of the other characters.
