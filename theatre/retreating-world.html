The Retreating World 2017

Play

Theatre Workshop (Black Box Theatre), University of Tehran

Director
Sina Aghazade
Writer
Naomi Wallace
Actors
Hamidreza Mohammadi, Reihane Baraghani, Sahand Khalili, Mehrnoush Alizade
Assistant Directors
Mana Mogharabin, Shervin Zarkalam

The Retreating World was a monologue performed by four actors. Rather than being embodied by a single character, the protagonist of the play was divided into four individuals, regardless of gender. In this interpretation, the monologue became a shared voice belonging to different people, with different genders and identities.

The original text tells the story of a young Iraqi man reflecting on the impact of war on his life. The performance sought to move his words beyond the experience of a single character and extend them to a wider collective. By having four actors embody the same character, the personal account of war was transformed into a shared human experience.
