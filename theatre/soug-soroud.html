Soug Soroud was a shadow and puppet performance that told the story of a musician who returns to the old bazaar after it has been destroyed by an airstrike, playing his instrument among the ruins.

The performance took place inside a large cube enclosed by translucent white fabric. The audience entered the structure and became immersed within the performance space, while the performers’ silhouettes were cast across its surrounding walls, creating a shifting interplay of shadow, movement, and sound.

The piece was selected as the Best Performance in the Performance Section of the 17th University Theatre Experience Festival.

One of the main production challenges was constructing a large-scale cube that could be safely assembled and dismantled with ease. The framework was therefore constructed using PVC water pipes, providing a lightweight, modular, and practical structure for the installation.
