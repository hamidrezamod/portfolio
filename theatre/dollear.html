Dollear was the final project for the Advanced Acting and Directing class, presented as part of a repertory alongside other student works.

The performance was a dramaturgical adaptation of Shakespeare’s King Lear. The central concept was to portray Lear’s daughters as wind-up dolls, while relocating the story from its original historical period to the contemporary world.

The performers and audience members were seated together around a single table, creating an intimate performance setting in which the boundary between the audience and the performers was deliberately blurred.
