Relingo 2022

Interactive Performance

Azadi St, Tehran

Director
Maryam Hashem Pour
Designers
Maryam Hashem Pour, Ali Kargari
Assistant Director
Hamid Reza Mohammadi
Graphic Designer
Zahra Doost

Wandering through Relingo is a search through a reservoir of possibilities. It is an attempt to claim a temporary, imagined sense of ownership over a placeless space, to reconstruct whatever place has been lost.

The performance guides each audience member individually along a path. After a period of wandering through Relingo, it offers them a space of their own to construct and reimagine.
