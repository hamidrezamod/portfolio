Bi 2018

Installation Art

Faculty of Fine Arts, University of Tehran

Designer
Hamid Reza Mohammadi

Bi was an installation combining ice and helium-filled balloons, presented in the grounds of the University of Tehran’s College of Fine Arts, beneath its main covered corridor connecting different university buildings.

The strings attached to the balloons were embedded at different depths within blocks of ice. At different intervals, the balloons were released as the ice gradually melted, causing the installation to continuously change in form and position.

The work explored the simultaneous concepts of the passage of time, transformation, and displacement. As people moved through and occupied the university grounds, they encountered the installation in different states, making its transformation part of their everyday experience of the space.
