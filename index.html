About Me

It’s strange, I know, to start with the weather. But I love cloudy days... a soft rain, a little mist, the smell of wet grass, and a slow, cold wind. Maybe because this kind of weather has always been a character in my creativity. It’s dramatic enough to hold love, fear, sadness, joy, and curiosity.

I’m Hamid Reza, born in 1997. I studied Acting & Directing for my BA and Cinema for my MA. My main interests are design, dramaturgy, and object theatre. I write, direct, and make short films, exploring stories across different forms and mediums.
